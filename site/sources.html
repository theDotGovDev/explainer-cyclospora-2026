Cyclosporiasis is treated with prescription antibiotics; if you have watery diarrhea lasting more than a few days, seek care. Seek care urgently for signs of dehydration, bloody stool, high fever, or if you are immunocompromised, pregnant, very young or elderly.

  8. Individual risk varies substantially. These are population-average estimates. People who are immunocompromised, pregnant, elderly or very young face materially higher risk of severe illness, and both deaths in this outbreak were in people with significant underlying conditions.   11. Do not read this as a reason to avoid fruits and vegetables. The health benefits of produce substantially outweigh these risks for nearly everyone.
